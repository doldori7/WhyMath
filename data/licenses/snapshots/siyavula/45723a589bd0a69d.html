Leaderboards

  1. By opting in, you agree that your first name, last name, grade group and school name (along with your current rank) will be displayed publicly on our site to celebrate your mastery and progress.
  2. For learners under 18, you confirm that you have obtained the consent of your parent or guardian to participate and have your details displayed publicly. If you do not have this consent, you may not opt in.

  9. Siyavula reserves the right to remove any user from the leaderboard for behavior that undermines the fairness of the rankings.
